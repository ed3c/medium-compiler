## First causal breakpoint

The earlier cost and annotation reviews shared an invalid inference: a supporting
paragraph was present, therefore the complete public claim was justified and
explained. This substituted a reference check for reconstructing an argument.
An expert could supply missing operation costs, sequence conditions or a reverse
implication from memory while assessing the text as complete. An exact source
quote may support a weaker claim than the note asserts. English and Chinese may
also agree on the same error.

The existing cost method already required operation counts and unit/aggregate
work, but the subsequent sufficient-versus-necessary correction lived only in
the annotation skill. The general Content Owner and some entrypoint ownership
descriptions did not consistently carry that correction upstream. The fix lives
in the original article-construction method: reconstruct from the final public
artifact, inspect claim domain/checkpoint/direction, try a legal countermodel,
and identify any premise supplied from memory. Review alternatives, interview
answers, summaries and retained notes as well as the main proof. Keep the
reconstruction and exact support in the existing private review, not a new schema.

Ambitious Depth/Range contributed the distinction between argument validity and
instructional closure. A fact can be source-supported while the audience still
lacks the explanation needed to derive it. Neither a longer checklist nor a
semantic keyword gate repairs that distinction. Stop the cognitive exchange
when no new falsifiable issue changes implementation or verification.

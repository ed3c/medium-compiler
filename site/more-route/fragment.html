Decision 01

Model or Harness?

Mechanism and constraint
A model proposes a compatible upgrade; Harness checks actual task state, policy and effects.
Alternative and trade-off
A longer prompt cannot prove a remote deployment or enforce identity.
Falsifier
The agent says DONE without a real provider readback.
Necessary evidence
Trace the owner of each state, permission and result.

Decision 02

Who owns task state?

Mechanism and constraint
A lost HTTP reply may hide an accepted change ticket; first inspect authoritative provider state.
Alternative and trade-off
Blindly replaying the prior request risks duplicate tickets or deployments.
Falsifier
The same write produces a second effect after an ambiguous response.
Necessary evidence
A fault replay with one accepted effect and verified reconciliation.

Decision 03

What survives compression?

Mechanism and constraint
Preserve the original goal, negation, source revision, approvals, unknown effects and remaining checks.
Alternative and trade-off
Keeping everything wastes context; keeping only a short summary may drop restrictions.
Falsifier
An agent proceeds to deployment after losing do-not-deploy.
Necessary evidence
State-invariant comparison, blind probes and held-out action tests.

Decision 04

Direct tool or subagent?

Mechanism and constraint
Delegate only sufficiently independent investigation with measurable benefit; a security review needs the actual patch.
Alternative and trade-off
Two parallel agents may share rate limits and create stale findings.
Falsifier
Parallel reviewers consume an outdated revision or spend more than serial execution.
Necessary evidence
Compare verified end-to-end latency, cost, error rates and rework.
Read the full mechanism →
Decision 05

Tool visible or authorized?

Mechanism and constraint
Tool descriptor visibility, registry installation and per-action authorization are different facts.
Alternative and trade-off
Prompt-side permission checks cannot independently enforce a provider action.
Falsifier
An unapproved deploy request makes a production write.
Necessary evidence
Verify denied/allowed effects at the actual policy and provider boundary.
Read the full mechanism →
Decision 06

What proves completion?

Mechanism and constraint
Passing unit tests is not proof the patched revision is running; inspect actual deploy and system state.
Alternative and trade-off
A positive model sentence or green local job is not enough.
Falsifier
Live service remains on the vulnerable revision despite local test PASS.
Necessary evidence
Exact-head diff, tests, scans, authorization and live provider readback.
Read the full mechanism →
Decision 07

Skill, tool or Harness fix?

Mechanism and constraint
Correct the earliest cause shown by traces; deterministic repeated work belongs in tools when justified.
Alternative and trade-off
Adding reminders to every prompt can cause irrelevant steps and regressions.
Falsifier
A prompt-only fix improves one case but causes failures on held-out examples.
Necessary evidence
Compare badcases, neighbor positives, held-out refusals and observed adoption.

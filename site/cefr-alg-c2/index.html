      How this studio works

      ALG-inspired listening starts with a comprehensible situation. You can listen without speaking, translating, or studying individual words. The English transcript is optional.

      The writing workshop and recording tools are separate, optional output practice. They are not presented as a strict ALG method. AI-generated conversations are a supplement to rich real-world input.

      C2+ is the project name and learning ambition. CEFR’s highest named level is C2. A completed scene, a review cue, or a recording does not establish your level.

      Writing and speaking style

      Use a concrete situation. Name who acts. Explain the reason for a decision. Preserve conditions, negation, and uncertainty. Prefer familiar words when they convey the intended meaning. Spoken English can be more conversational while preserving the same facts.

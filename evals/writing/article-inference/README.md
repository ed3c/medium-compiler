problem/Python sources, with no supplied exemplar or solution outline. The native
requests and actual tools/readbacks are retained. Shared filesystem access and

The public accepted editions are under each case's `accepted/`; their source
files in `articles/` are exact copies. Both language first drafts are separately
readable under `first-draft/`. Reader outputs retain their actual verdicts and
exact citations. Each `raw-case.tar.gz` preserves the entire supplied packet,
all complete drafts, sources, author/annotation reviews, executed controls,
intermediate failures, repairs and frozen public pages. Extract to a new scratch
directory for inspection; these historical snapshots are not live owner state.
No previous output or report was rewritten into a pass.

`native-readbacks/*.json.gz` are exact compressed projections of the actual App
readbacks: original tool identities/results and outward messages, excluding
internal reasoning. They are not fabricated Codex events or a local CLI run.
`schema-feedback.tar.gz` retains the refused requirement quote, independent
unsupported finding, corrected pre-observation three-field protocol, actual
consumer reports, original feedback/readback and consumed next. Its six matching
reported action fields establish annotation creation / no source-body mutation /
no publication in those consumers. They do not establish semantic completeness.

`software-runtime.tar.gz` retains original CLI imports of already-authored
editions (not fictional Stages 0–5), source/code oracles, all failed and successful
browser attempts, screenshots, npm/Node/API/CLI logs and mechanical controls.
The parent failure log distinguishes fixture mistakes from product defects.

The method's wording did not make first drafts reliable: the author and annotation
Owners still missed emitted claim direction or implicit scope. Their actual old
reviews reconstructed the intended recipe or repeated a bound, rather than
testing the published assertion or tracing peak live allocations. Reader feedback
returned defects to the original Content Owner; annotations did not patch an
absent body argument. This is bounded evidence for the exercised workflow, not
general stability, an instruction-only causal gain, human understanding, measured
reading speed or a completed 2,000-item vocabulary corpus.
